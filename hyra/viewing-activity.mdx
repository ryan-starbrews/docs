## How to View Your Activity

Viewing your in-game activity through Hyra is very simple. 

- To view your activity, head over to the [Activity Portal](https://app.hyra.io/69716626a0dddd4afa38f8c6/activity/history) in Hyra. This will show you all of the time you spend in-game and the number of messages you sent in-game

<Info>
  If you believe your activity was incorrectly tracked, open a [Support Ticket](https://docs.starbrews.cafe/ticket) and provide evidence of you being in-game when you believe that your activity was incorrectly tracked.
</Info>
