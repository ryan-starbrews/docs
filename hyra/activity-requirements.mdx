## Managing Activity

Here at StarBrews, we use [Hyra](https:/docs.starbrews.cafe/hyra) to manage and track activity. All staff members must remain active, even if you don't have a requirement. If you need to go inactive for any period of time where you cannot meet a activity requierment, please look at how you can create a [Leave of Absence](/hyra/loa) form.
