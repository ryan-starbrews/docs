## How to fill out a Leave of Absence

Filling out a Leave of Absence request through Hyra is a simple process that can be done within minutes.

- To begin the LOA request, head over to the [LOA Request Portal](https://app.hyra.io/69716626a0dddd4afa38f8c6/activity/inactivity) and select the start and end dates of your LOA.
- 
- After selecting the dates of your LOA, choose one of the following reasons for your LOA from the drop-down menu.
  - \<Icon icon="bandage" color="#006241" /\> Medical 
  - \<Icon icon="head-side-brain" color="#006241" /\> Mental Health 
  - \<Icon icon="book-user" color="#006241" /\> Personal Reasons
  - \<Icon icon="umbrella-beach" color="#006241" /\> Holiday/Vacation
  - \<Icon icon="truck-moving" color="#006241" /\> Moving Houses
  - \<Icon icon="school" color="#006241" /\> Education
  - \<Icon icon="cross" color="#006241" /\> Religious 
  - \<Icon icon="tombstone" color="#006241" /\> Bereavement
  - \<Icon icon="jet-fighter" color="#006241" /\> Military
  - \<Icon icon="rectangles-mixed" color="#006241" /\> Other (requires an explanation)
- After selecting a reason for your LOA, you can add a note. If you choose not to explain, please put "N/A."

  <Danger>
    We have the right to decline a LOA request if we believe that there is no valid reason for a LOA.

    Taking this LOA request form unseriously may result in disciplinary actions.
  </Danger>
