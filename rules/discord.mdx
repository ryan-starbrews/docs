---
title: "Discord Guidelines"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="NSFW">
    This server is a safe space for all people who are 13 and above, all content in the server must remain SFW and appropriate for all members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spam">
    Do not spam in any of our channels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Underage Users">
    All users must be 13 years of age or above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No Leaking or Doxxing">
    Leaking personal information, such as social media profiles, addresses, phone numbers, full names and such will not be tolerated. This applies to members of the server, aswell as people who might be playing the cafe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No Hating">
    Some things that fall under this category are racism, ableism, politics, xenophobia, homophobia, etc. You can talk about such topics respectfully but being in support of said things will result in punishment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No Advertising">
    Advertising of any kind is strictly prohibited; if you want to parter with us please open a ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Channels Correctly">
    Use all channels for the correct purposes, failure to do so can result in disciplinary actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Common Sense">
    Use common sense when conducting yourself in the server. Before doing something you may think could be bad, ask yourself “How would a stranger react to this?” If the answer is negatively, don’t do it.
  </Step>
</Steps>
